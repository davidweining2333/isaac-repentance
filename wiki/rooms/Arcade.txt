An Arcade is a special room that can only spawn on an even-numbered floor (Basement II, Caves II, Depths II, Womb II) if you have 5 coins or more when you leave the previous floor. Arcades require one coin to be opened. They can be replaced with a Vault if Isaac is carrying two keys or more.

Arcades cannot spawn on XL floors.
 Unless it is on an alt path floor.
 Arcades can spawn on the first floor of the alternate chapters (Downpour I, Mines I, Mausoleum I, Corpse I).

== Trivia ==
The posters that can appear on the arcade's walls are pictures of games that Edmund McMillen has worked on, such as Gish, Super Meat Boy, and Time Fcuk.
In the original Binding of Isaac, the arcade's icon was a 6-sided dice. In Rebirth, this icon was changed to a joystick.
